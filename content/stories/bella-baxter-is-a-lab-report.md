If any modern role was designed for ActorRating’s five sliders, it’s Bella Baxter.

Physical comedy, moral improvisation, voice work that evolves like a time-lapse — Stone makes technical skill visible without turning it into a stunt reel.

## Why Bella breaks one-number scoring

Bella is not a single character state. She is a sequence of states with continuity. Quick rate can still work as a gut check, but the five criteria are where the role becomes articulate:

- **Technical Skill** — gait, voice, comic precision
- **Emotional Range** — appetite, fury, tenderness without reset buttons
- **Believability** — absurd premise, concrete person
- **Presence** — she steals oxygen without asking
- **Chemistry** — every counterpart becomes a different experiment

## The anti-trap

Do not rate the production design and call it Stone. Do not rate “feminist text” and call it a performance score. Rate whether she makes curiosity feel dangerous and joy feel political without lecturing you.

Stone’s Bella is a lab report you can argue with — which is the highest compliment a scoreboard can give.
