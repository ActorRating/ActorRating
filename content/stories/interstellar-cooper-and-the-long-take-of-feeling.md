Space movies recruit awe. Cooper recruits regret.

McConaughey’s best beats aren’t the docking porn — they’re the messages that arrive too late. If your score is mostly nostalgia for the Hans Zimmer drops, try again muted.

## Separate the instruments

*Interstellar* will try to make you rate cosmology. ActorRating wants the father performance: competence cracking, love turning into a physics problem, the shame of leaving and the impossibility of returning on time.

## Practical scoring

Watch once for spectacle if you must. Score on a second pass — or at least after the awe hangover. **Emotional Range** and **Believability** should lead. Presence will get free points from Nolan’s frames; spend them carefully.

Cooper is a grief performance wearing an astronaut suit. Rate the grief.
