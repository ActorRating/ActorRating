You just finished rating a performance. What should pop up next?

Not a random deep-cut from 1973. The cast you were already thinking about.

1. **Other leads** from the movie you just rated
2. **Supporting** turns from that same film
3. **More of the same actor** if those run out

Self credits, archive footage, and featurettes stay out.

## Why this order

Rating is momentum. If you just scored Damon’s Odysseus, your brain is already holding Holland, Zendaya, Hathaway. The feed should catch that heat — same for a Spider-Man unlock night when MJ and Ned and whoever rearranged Peter are sitting in your short-term memory.

Random discovery has a place. It is not the first screen after a score. First we finish the movie you are already arguing with.
