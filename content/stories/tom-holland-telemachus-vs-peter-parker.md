Tom Holland spent years making Spider-Man feel like the most sincere person in the room. *The Odyssey* and *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* arrive close enough together to test whether that sincerity is a tool — or a default.

## Telemachus: sincerity without a safety net

In Nolan’s epic, Holland’s Telemachus is not “young hero energy.” He is a political orphan with a famous last name. The suitors do not need him to be impressive. They need him to be manageable.

That changes the acting assignment. Quips become dangerous. Charm becomes a tell. Watch for:

- **Voice** that has practiced authority and still cracks when Odysseus becomes real
- **Physical smallness** next to Damon that is character, not just blocking
- **Anger** that is not teen franchise rebellion, but inheritance rage — *why did your myth cost me a childhood?*

If Holland plays Telemachus like Peter Parker in sandals, the score should drop. If he finds a quieter, harder boy — the kind who has been host for too long — the scorecard opens up.

## Peter Parker: sincerity after erasure

*Brand New Day* (July 31 wide) pushes the opposite loneliness. Peter is hyper-visible as Spider-Man and invisible as Peter. Friends moved on. The city wants the suit. The man inside the suit is negotiating with a version of himself that only the audience fully remembers.

This is where Holland’s sincerity can become extraordinary — or sentimental.

Rate **Believability** on whether the loneliness feels administrative (forms, habits, empty apartments) rather than speech-ready. Rate **Emotional Range** on whether he can play obligation without sainthood. Rate **Technical Skill** on the mask work *and* the unmasked work: the face that has to sell “I am fine being forgotten” while the plot proves he is not.

## Side-by-side prompts

After both films:

1. Which character is more afraid of being known?
2. Which performance uses silence better?
3. Where does Holland’s likability help — and where does it sand off the edge you needed?
4. If you could only keep one score on ActorRating, which turn taught you more about him?

## Don’t let Marvel gravity flatten Odyssey

Prestige films sometimes get automatic “serious actor” inflation. Franchises get automatic discount. ActorRating’s whole point is to refuse both.

Telemachus can be a 9 in a movie you only kind of liked. Peter can be a 6 in a movie that prints money. Or the reverse. The number is for the work, not the marketing category.

Holland asked for a delay so he could do Nolan first. That choice only matters if the performances feel like two different instruments — not one brand extension with better costumes.
