*The Odyssey* is no longer a trailer. It’s a film you can actually argue about.

That means the interesting question isn’t “did Nolan cook?” — it’s whether Matt Damon’s Odysseus holds as a **performance**. Epic scale will inflate movie scores. ActorRating exists so the acting doesn’t get lost inside the spectacle.

## What to score first

- Screen presence when the myth gets loud
- Believability when Odysseus is just a man delaying home
- Chemistry with the ensemble that keeps rewriting his fate

If your number is mostly “the ships looked insane,” try again. Rate the turn. Leave the IMAX flex for the film score.
