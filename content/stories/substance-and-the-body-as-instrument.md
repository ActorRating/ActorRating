Horror loves metaphor. Moore plays the metaphor like a concert violinist who also wants to survive the concert.

Rate technical skill without reducing the role to practical-effects tourism.

## What Moore is actually doing

She is playing fame’s body politics as lived panic — not a thinkpiece with fake blood. The performance has to make vanity feel rational from the inside, then make the rational collapse.

## Criteria map

- **Technical Skill** — physical comedy adjacent to body horror; precision under prosthetic chaos
- **Emotional Range** — hunger, humiliation, rage, pathetic hope
- **Believability** — would this person take the deal?
- **Presence** — can she hold your eye when the movie wants you staring at the effect?

If your number is mostly “gross movie slap,” you graded the genre. Rate Moore. The gore is the set dressing for a career-risk turn.
