Anticipation is not a performance score.

Unreleased titles stay behind **Coming soon** until release day — because a rating without a performance is just branding. Trailers lie. Marketing lies harder. Early “scores” poison the board before craft exists.

## What the gate protects

- No trailer-based inflation for Holland/Zendaya before *Brand New Day* unlocks
- No prestige hype laundering for films you have not seen
- A clean unlock moment when the acting is actually fair game

## What you can do instead

Rate the turns that already exist. Build Chalamet’s Paul on *Dune: Part Two*. Build Holland’s prior Peters if you want franchise context. Build Zendaya’s Tashi / Chani work if you want her control vocabulary.

When a title unlocks, the argument can start with receipts instead of hype. That is not gatekeeping for sport. That is how a scoreboard stays honest.
