Anticipation is not a performance score.

*Dune: Part Three* is on the calendar. ActorRating still shows **Coming soon** until release day — because a rating without a performance is just branding. Trailers lie. Marketing lies harder.

## What you can do instead

Rate Chalamet’s Paul in *Dune: Part Two*. Build the scoreboard that already exists. When Part Three unlocks, the argument can start with receipts instead of hype.
