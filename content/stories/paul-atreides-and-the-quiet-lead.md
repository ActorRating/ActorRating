Desert epics reward posters. ActorRating rewards the person inside the myth.

Chalamet’s Paul often *receives* the world instead of punching through it. That can look passive if you’re scoring sandworms. Score the arc, not the set piece.

## Quiet is not empty

A quiet lead is doing invisible labor: absorbing prophecy, negotiating inheritance, deciding when violence becomes identity. If you only reward actors who project outward, you will systematically underrate Chalamet’s Paul — and a whole tradition of watchful protagonists.

## How to rate him fairly

1. Track whether the performance **hardens** across *Part Two* without becoming a different person overnight.
2. Separate messianic imagery from the acting that sells reluctance, appetite, and political coldness.
3. Use **Emotional Range** and **Believability** before Presence — Presence will get charity from Villeneuve’s frames.

Paul is a leadership performance disguised as a destiny performance. Rate the leadership.
