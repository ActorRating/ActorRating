A scene-stealer is a supporting performance that behaves like a lead in your memory.

ActorRating’s tiers exist so those turns can be found without pretending every credited extra is the same discovery problem.

## Steal vs inflate

Not every loud entrance is a steal. A real scene-stealer rearranges what the movie is about while you are still watching — Pitt’s Tyler, Simmons’ Fletcher, Bardem’s Chigurh energy. The billing may say supporting. Your nervous system files an appeal.

## How to score them

Rate the turn on its own page. Do not average it into the lead out of guilt. Do not punish it for “taking over” — that is often the craft succeeding.

If a supporting 9 sits next to a lead 6 in the same film, the scoreboard is doing journalism.
