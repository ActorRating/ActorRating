## Heath Ledger in The Dark Knight

Ledger's Joker is theatrical anarchy with a craftsman's precision — the smeared makeup, the voice that sounds borrowed from a damaged instrument, the sense that every scene tilts toward him even when he's not speaking. On ActorRating, this is one of the clearest community favorites in the cohort: people aren't just nostalgia-voting Batman; they're scoring a supporting performance that hijacked a blockbuster.

If you've only experienced this Joker through memes, go rate the five dimensions yourself. Chaos is easy to quote. Harder to score honestly once you separate it from the movie's legend.

## Joaquin Phoenix in Joker

Phoenix's Arthur Fleck is intimate where Ledger was centrifugal — a man dissolving in public, not a terrorist philosophizing in a hospital. The Community Rating here has been brutal in its clarity: audiences who show up for the performance tend to go high. Whether that reflects the work or the cultural moment is exactly the kind of debate ActorRating is built to host with numbers instead of quote-tweets.
