**Stories** are visual, timely, performance-forward. **News** is journal voice — criteria, product philosophy, how to read the numbers. Same team. Different tempo.

## How to read the rails

- Stories: Damon / Holland / Zendaya week, scene-stealers, “rate the acting not the movie”
- News: Coming soon policy, guest ratings, community vs TMDB, five sliders

When a release week hits hard — *The Odyssey* out, *Brand New Day* days away — Stories carry the heat and News keeps the rules legible. You need both or the site becomes either a hype blog or a manual nobody opens.
