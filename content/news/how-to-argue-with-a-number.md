If the community sits at 8.1 and you sat at 5.5, congratulations: you found the product. Open the five criteria. Find the slider where you diverged.

## A cleaner fight

Bad argument: “You’re all wrong.”  
Better argument: “Believability carried this for people; Technical Skill didn’t land for me.”

Micro-reviews that name a criterion are how a scoreboard becomes a conversation instead of a pile of unexplained floats. Farrell’s quiet devastation in *Banshees* is a perfect lab for this — people can love the film and still split hard on the turn’s temperature.
