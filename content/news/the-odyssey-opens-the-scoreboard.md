*The Odyssey* is out. That matters here for one boring, important reason: **you can rate it now**.

Until release day, ActorRating keeps unreleased titles behind Coming soon. No fake early averages. No trailer-based score inflation.

## How to use the page

- Start with quick rate if you want a gut number tonight
- Open the five criteria if the epic is drowning the craft
- Compare community acting scores to the film-level TMDB signal — different questions on purpose
- Leave a micro-review that names one criterion, not one adjective

## Who to put on the board first

Damon’s Odysseus is the obvious headline. Don’t stop there.

- **Holland / Telemachus** — yearning without quips
- **Zendaya / Athena** — divine strategy without romance default
- **Hathaway / Penelope** — the cost of waiting, played as character rather than symbol

The supporting cage match around Damon is where the scoreboard gets interesting — and where “great movie” opinions tend to launder weak turns.

## A note on the week ahead

Holland and Zendaya are about to reappear in *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*. That makes Odyssey ratings more valuable, not less: you want clean numbers *before* the franchise discourse contaminates the prestige discourse (and vice versa).

Damon’s Odysseus is the center of gravity. Log it like you mean it.
