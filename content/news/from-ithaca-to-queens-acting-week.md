Most summers ask you to pick a lane: prestige or popcorn. This week asks you to keep **two scoreboards open at once**.

*The Odyssey* is out — Matt Damon’s Odysseus is fair game, and so is the ensemble cage match around him (Holland’s Telemachus, Zendaya’s Athena, Hathaway’s Penelope, and the rest of Nolan’s stacked deck).

*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* is days from wide release (July 31). Same Holland. Same Zendaya. Completely different acting physics.

## The ActorRating itinerary

**Already unlocked**

- Rate Damon as Odysseus if you have seen it — protect the number from IMAX inflation.
- Rate Holland’s Telemachus as a son performance, not a “he’s in Nolan now” novelty.
- Rate Zendaya’s Athena as divine strategy, not celebrity cameo energy.

**About to unlock**

- Rate Holland’s Peter under the erasure premise.
- Rate Zendaya’s MJ under the memory gap.
- Only then run the side-by-side.

## Why “both movies” is the editorial, not the spoiler

Because the culture will talk about scheduling and dating and premiere optics. Fine. The useful conversation is craft:

- Can Holland play two lonely young men without repeating the same earnestness patch?
- Can Zendaya play omniscient power and amnesiac aftermath without flattening either into “iconic”?
- Can Damon still be the emotional center of an epic when the internet wants to make the supporting cast the main story?

## Community challenge

If you rate more than one of these turns this week, leave a micro-review that names **one criterion** that decided your number. “It was awesome” is not a scoreboard. “Chemistry collapsed when the god scenes turned romantic” is.

ActorRating exists for that second sentence.

See you on the other side of July 31 — with cleaner numbers than the timeline.
