ActorRating lets guests rate a few performances before asking for an account. Your guest ratings live locally until you join.

## Why the free pass exists

Because the first score should be about the turn on screen — not a signup wall. Try the slider. Feel the five criteria. Decide if this scoreboard is for you.

## What changes when you join

- Your ratings sync to a profile
- You can leave micro-reviews
- You enter the community average properly
- Invites / waitlist rules apply when the gate is on

Guest mode is a runway, not a loophole forever. When we ask you to join, it is so the scoreboard can remember you — and so your Odysseus take still exists next month.
